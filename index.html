Frequently Asked Questions

What are the two subscription options?

Grade Access costs 100 EGP per month and unlocks the selected school grade. The separate Mr. Fluent English Course costs 100 EGP every 30 days and includes the A1–C2 English course. They are independent memberships; paying for one does not activate the other.

How do I activate Grade Access?

Create or log in to your student account, choose one of the supported school grades, submit the payment request, send the shown Mobile Cash amount, and upload a clear receipt with the sender number. Access opens only after the admin approves the payment.

How do I activate Mr. Fluent English Course?

Log in to your student account, choose Mr. Fluent English Course for 100 EGP per 30 days, follow the payment instructions, and upload the receipt. The placement test and course levels remain locked until the admin approves the payment.

Which school grades are supported?

1st, 2nd and 3rd Preparatory plus 1st, 2nd and 3rd Secondary. Grade Access applies only to the grade you subscribe to.

How long does access last?

Grade Access lasts for the period shown on its subscription, normally one month. Mr. Fluent English Course lasts 30 days. Check Account → Overview to see the current status and expiry date; renew through the site when needed.

What happens after I upload a receipt?

Your request shows as waiting for review until the admin checks it. Uploading a receipt does not activate access automatically. If a request is rejected, check the status or admin note and follow the payment instructions before submitting again.

Where is my learning progress stored?

Core course records such as completed lessons, quiz attempts, homework submissions and certificates are linked to your student account. Extra browser-based items such as saved lessons, lesson notes and study-plan activity can be merged between devices using the progress sync controls in Account after the progress-sync database setup is installed.

How do I sync my progress to another device?

On the device that has your latest browser-based progress, log in and open Account → Overview, then choose Sync this device. On the second device, log in to the same account and choose Restore & merge from account. This combines data rather than deleting the other device's saved progress. Cloud sync is already configured for DOMA.

Can I study from my phone?

Yes. DOMA works on mobile and desktop browsers. Sign in to the same student account to access account-based learning records on another device.
