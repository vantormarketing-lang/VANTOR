Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start learning?

Create a student account, choose your grade, select monthly or annual access, send the payment through Mobile Cash, and upload the receipt. Access starts after admin approval.

Which grades are supported?

1st, 2nd and 3rd Preparatory plus 1st, 2nd and 3rd Secondary.

What can I study?

Published content can include grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, quizzes and homework for your selected grade.

Where do I see my progress?

Use the Student Dashboard to track completed lessons, quiz attempts, certificates, homework and notifications.

Can I study from my phone?

Yes. The student interface is responsive and works on desktop and mobile browsers.
