Units & Lessons

Students can preview the structure here; the full protected lesson content opens after active access.

6 units36 lessons6 quizzes8 homework
1
Unit 1: Life in a Digital WorldDigital life, healthy technology habits and everyday routines.
⌄
Lesson 1: A Day in My Digital Lifelesson

Understand a short blog-style text about daily digital habits and identify the main idea and details.

Lesson 2: Digital Vocabularyvocabulary

Build a practical vocabulary set for devices, apps and online actions.

Lesson 3: Present Simple and Adverbs of Frequencygrammar

Use the present simple and adverbs of frequency to talk about routines.

Lesson 4: The Smart Classroomreading

Read and understand a short passage about responsible technology use at school.

Lesson 5: My Digital Routinewriting

Write a short, organized paragraph about a normal digital day.

Lesson 6: Listening — A School Day Onlinelistening

Listen for specific details about when and why a student uses technology.

2
Unit 2: Learning to LearnStudy skills, learning tools, asking for help and planning revision.
⌄
Lesson 1: How I Learnlesson

Understand different ways of learning and choose useful study habits.

Lesson 2: Learning Toolsvocabulary

Learn vocabulary for books, notes, flashcards and digital learning tools.

Lesson 3: Possession and Requestsgrammar

Use possessive adjectives and simple can/will requests in classroom situations.

Lesson 4: My Study Planreading

Read a simple weekly study plan and identify priorities and free time.

Lesson 5: Good Study Habitswriting

Write a clear weekly English study plan using time expressions and goals.

Lesson 6: Listening — Before the Testlistening

Listen for sequence and identify what a student does before an English test.

3
Unit 3: Role ModelsPeople who inspire us, their actions, achievements and qualities.
⌄
Lesson 1: People Who Inspire Uslesson

Talk about qualities and actions that make a person a useful role model.

Lesson 2: Role Model Vocabularyvocabulary

Build vocabulary for qualities, achievements and community action.

Lesson 3: Present Continuous and Pronounsgrammar

Describe actions happening now and use subject/object pronouns correctly.

Lesson 4: A Young Volunteerreading

Read about a student volunteer and identify actions, qualities and reasons for helping.

Lesson 5: My Role Modelwriting

Write a short paragraph describing a person you admire and what they are doing now.

Lesson 6: Listening — An Interview with a Role Modellistening

Listen to an interview and identify personal information, qualities and current actions.

4
Unit 4: Smart ChoicesHealthy, safe and responsible choices at school and at home.
⌄
Lesson 1: Smart Everyday Choiceslesson

Understand the idea of making healthy, safe and responsible choices.

Lesson 2: Smart Choices Vocabularyvocabulary

Learn vocabulary related to health, safety, recycling and saving resources.

Lesson 3: Imperatives and Zero Conditionalgrammar

Give instructions with imperatives and explain general results with the zero conditional.

Lesson 4: If We Recycle...reading

Read about recycling habits and identify actions and their results.

Lesson 5: My Smart Choiceswriting

Write a short practical guide with rules and zero-conditional results.

Lesson 6: Listening — Making a Good Choicelistening

Listen to a short conversation and identify the problem, the choice and the result.

5
Unit 5: Future DreamsJobs, talents, future plans and choosing a useful path.
⌄
Lesson 1: Dreams for the Futurelesson

Talk about future goals, jobs and the skills needed to achieve them.

Lesson 2: Jobs and Skillsvocabulary

Learn vocabulary for careers, talents and personal skills.

Lesson 3: Comparatives, Superlatives and Articlesgrammar

Compare people and things and use a/an/the in simple contexts.

Lesson 4: Choosing the Right Careerreading

Read a short text about matching interests and skills with future plans.

Lesson 5: My Future Planwriting

Write a personal future plan using reasons, skills and target actions.

Lesson 6: Listening — Future Planslistening

Listen for future goals and the steps students plan to take.

6
Unit 6: Global CitizenCommunity, responsibility, cooperation and living in a connected world.
⌄
Lesson 1: One Worldlesson

Understand what it means to be a responsible member of a local and global community.

Lesson 2: Global Citizen Vocabularyvocabulary

Build vocabulary for community action, cooperation and cultural respect.

Lesson 3: Past-Simple Questionsgrammar

Ask and answer Wh- questions about completed actions in the past.

Lesson 4: Around the World, Togetherreading

Read about students working together on a community project.

Lesson 5: How I Can Help My Communitywriting

Write about practical actions you can take as a responsible citizen.

Lesson 6: Listening — A Global Teamlistening

Listen to a short report and identify people, actions, time and place.

Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start learning?

Create a student account, choose your grade, select monthly or annual access, send the payment through Mobile Cash, and upload the receipt. Access starts after admin approval.

Which grades are supported?

1st, 2nd and 3rd Preparatory plus 1st, 2nd and 3rd Secondary.

What can I study?

Published content can include grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, quizzes and homework for your selected grade.

Where do I see my progress?

Use the Student Dashboard to track completed lessons, quiz attempts, certificates, homework and notifications.

Can I study from my phone?

Yes. The student interface is responsive and works on desktop and mobile browsers.
